The split matters when these stand in for spot prices: the physically backed trusts track metal almost one-for-one, while the miner baskets carry equity risk and operating leverage, so they overshoot the commodity in both directions. LIT, URA, REMX and SETM are the practical proxies for lithium, uranium, tungsten and cobalt, none of which have a free spot feed.
