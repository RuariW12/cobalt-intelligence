prices & policy

inflation → interest rates → equities / credit

The central chain. Hot prints raise the expected path of rates; higher rates lower the value of distant earnings and widen spreads. Watch the 2-year, which moves on expectations before the Fed acts.

monetary policy → liquidity → financial markets

The slower version of the same mechanism, acting through the quantity of money rather than its price. Liquidity has no single series — read it off credit spreads and the dollar.

interest rates → housing → construction & industrial

Housing is the most rate-sensitive part of the economy and turns first. Housing starts lead industrial demand by quarters, so Caterpillar and Deere confirm what starts already said.

demand & growth

labor market → consumer spending → economic growth

Wages fund consumption, and consumption is most of GDP. Jobless claims turn well before the unemployment rate does — watch the weekly series, not the monthly one.

economic growth → industrial demand → commodities

Growth shows up as physical demand before it shows up in GDP, which is reported with a lag and revised. Commodity prices are the real-time version.

supply & materials

china & global manufacturing → industrial metals → commodity prices

China is the marginal buyer of most industrial metals, so copper reads as a China indicator more than a US one. Divergence between copper and US growth usually means the story is in Asia.

geopolitical events → energy & material supply → inflation

The chain that runs backwards through the first one: supply shocks raise prices without any demand strength behind them, which is why they are the hardest case for monetary policy.

ai investment → semiconductors → electricity demand → commodities

The newest chain and the least settled. Capex guidance is the upstream signal; uranium, natural gas and copper are where it lands. If the first link breaks, the rest unwinds in order.

Grey nodes have no page of their own yet. Each chain is also a route — following the links walks the mechanism in order.
