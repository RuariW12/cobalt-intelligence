## Personal Economics & Markets Tracker

The user is building a personal economics tracker designed to monitor major economic conditions, financial markets, commodities/materials, and important companies.

### 1. MACROECONOMICS

Track the overall health and direction of the economy.

* **Inflation**

  * CPI
  * Core CPI
  * PCE
  * Core PCE
  * PPI

* **Labor**

  * Unemployment rate
  * Nonfarm payrolls
  * Initial jobless claims
  * JOLTS job openings
  * Wage growth

* **Growth**

  * Real GDP
  * Retail sales
  * Industrial production
  * ISM Manufacturing PMI
  * ISM Services PMI
  * Housing starts

### 2. INTEREST RATES & CREDIT

Track monetary policy and the cost/availability of capital.

* Federal Funds Rate
* 2-Year Treasury
* 10-Year Treasury
* 30-Year Treasury
* 10Y–2Y yield spread
* Investment-grade credit spreads
* High-yield credit spreads

### Primary Purpose

The tracker should prioritize understanding **economic relationships and trends**, rather than functioning solely as an investment portfolio tracker.

Important relationships to observe include:

* Inflation → interest rates → equities/credit
* Labor market → consumer spending → economic growth
* Interest rates → housing → construction/industrial activity
* AI investment → semiconductors → electricity demand → commodities
* China/global manufacturing → industrial metals → commodity prices
* Geopolitical events → energy/material supply → inflation
* Monetary policy → liquidity → financial markets
* Economic growth → industrial demand → commodities
